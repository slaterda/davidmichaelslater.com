Screenplays

Fun & Games: Screenplay by Jay Lender (original SpongeBob writer/director) adapted from the novel: “The funniest book about growing up Jewish since Portnoy’s Complaint.” (Heeb Magazine) R-rated dysfunctional family dramedy.

Sparks: Dark YA adventure/fantasy/horror/mystery: Screenplay by Jay Lender: When a homebound seventeen-year-old orphan spies on the fairy-tale mansion across the road, its sixteen-year-old “princess” comes after him, looking for trouble. Before they know it, the pair is on the run, pursued by a seemingly invincible killer, who will stop at nothing to prevent them from discovering the truth about who lives at Wolfgang Law’s Great Home for Good Girls and Boys — and why.

Mocha Cola High, R-rated fantasy/sci-fi satirical series: Screenplay by David Michael Slater & David Taylor: A globe-dominating corporation is bent on eliminating independent thinkers from the population. (Heathers + Truman Show + Stepford Wives) Available: One sheet, feature version, and series version with pilot

The Georgia Diaries Multiple TV Series, Serialized Drama, based on fact. Screenplay by David Taylor based on a short story by David Michael Slater. The first season centers around John Wesley’s nearly ruinous attempt to become the spiritual father of the Georgia colony, where he got entangled in all kinds of sordid affairs before fleeing back to England, after which he changed the world by founding Methodism. Available: One sheet, pilot, and series description.
