A guide by reading taste

What are you in the mood for?

Find a starting point among David’s books. Each recommendation includes its audience and a quick note on tone.

Dark comedy ↓Suspense ↓Magic & mystery ↓Books & wordplay ↓Heart & imagination ↓Ideas & debate ↓

Dark comedy & complicated lives

For readers who like humor tangled up with family secrets, cruelty, and consequence.

Adults · novel

UGLY ↗

Dark · sprawling · satirical

Three generations of women confront orphanages, privilege, and the predatory world of modeling in a tragicomedy of defiance.

Adults · novel

Fun & Games ↗

Funny · painful · nostalgic

An ’80s coming-of-age story in which friendship and outrageous family antics collide with betrayal and loss.

Suspense with a speculative edge

For readers drawn to dangerous secrets, desperate escapes, and an extraordinary twist on reality.

Teens · novel

Sparks ↗

Fast · mysterious · speculative

Two teenagers go on the run to uncover what lies behind a seemingly idyllic home for children.

Teens & adults · novel

The Vanishing ↗

Intense · historical · magical realism

A Jewish girl becomes invisible after a traumatic loss and uses her newfound power to resist the Nazis.

Reader note: Holocaust themes and traumatic loss. May not suit younger or highly sensitive readers.

Magic, mystery & adventure

For readers who want hidden powers, strange creatures, and a world bigger than it first appears.

Teens · series opener

The Book of Nonsense ↗

Mysterious · fantastical · high stakes

An unreadable old book draws twins Dexter and Daphna into a struggle over Words of Power. Start the seven-volume Forbidden Books series here.

Children · early chapter book

Bigfoot! ↗

Funny · mysterious · adventurous

The Mattigan siblings search for a creature their famous father insists cannot exist. The first of six Mysterious Monsters adventures.

Children · early chapter book

A Dire Situation ↗

Oddball · funny · action-packed

When the usual heroes fail, a family with very unusual superpowers steps in. Meet the Super Doopers in A Dire Situation.

Books, words & playful invention

For readers who enjoy language doing surprising things—and stories about the books themselves.

Adults · short stories

Dictionaries Out of Order ↗

Surreal · comic · philosophical

Intersecting stories set around Portland’s City of Books explore the strange power of words, from farce to philosophy.

Children · picture book

Seven Ate Nine ↗

Silly · pun-filled · mischievous

One is having a terrible day—and things get worse when Seven eats Nine. A counting story full of verbal and numerical mischief.

Children · wordless picture book

The Boy & the Book ↗

Playful · affectionate · visual

A library book tries to escape an enthusiastic little boy in a story told entirely through pictures.

Heart, humor & imagination

For young readers finding courage, making friends, or learning how to do the right thing.

Children · picture book

Bear With Me ↗

Warm · funny · reassuring

Max brings his stuffed bear to face the first day of school, where things get wild and an unexpected friendship offers help.

Children · picture book

Cheese Louise! ↗

Whimsical · adventurous · encouraging

A cheese who doubts herself becomes the unlikely hero of a refrigerator rescue, with plenty of food puns along the way.

Children · early chapter book

The Mysterious Mr. Mensch ↗

Magical · thoughtful · adventurous

Climb aboard Mr. Mensch’s magical Meshugenahmobile for adventures that explore ethics and the choices we make.

Ideas that invite an argument

For readers who enjoy questioning assumptions about schools, politics, and public life.

Adults · nonfiction

We’re Doing It Wrong ↗

Questioning · practical · reform-minded

A teacher examines 25 familiar ideas in education, asking what fails students and how schools could change.

Adults · nonfiction

Wingnuts ↗

Provocative · humorous · analytical

An examination of everyday extremism that challenges readers across the political spectrum to rethink how they argue and what they believe.
