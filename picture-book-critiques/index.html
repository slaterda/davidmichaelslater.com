Contact me first to check availability.

01

Comments on your manuscript

Specific observations and suggested edits addressing clarity, voice, word choice, word economy, pacing, and how the text sounds when read aloud.

02

A substantive editorial letter

Usually 1–3 pages exploring your story’s strengths, its main challenges, and practical revision priorities. I’ll consider the concept, characters, story arc, ending, intended age group, page-turn opportunities, and the space you leave for illustrations.

03

A second look after you revise

Send me your revised manuscript for fresh annotations and a shorter written assessment of what has improved and what still needs attention.

04

A 30-minute conversation

One phone or Zoom conversation to discuss the feedback and your next steps. Use it after either round, whenever it would help you most.

05

A follow-up email exchange

One exchange after the second round to clarify any remaining questions about the critique.

How we’ll work together

  1. Get in touch

    Tell me your manuscript’s title, word count, intended age group, and what you’d most like help with. I’ll confirm availability and send submission and payment details.

  2. Read, reflect, revise

    You’ll receive your annotated manuscript and editorial letter within two weeks of our agreed start date. Take time to consider the feedback and make your revisions.

  3. Take the next step

    Return your revised manuscript within 90 days of the first critique. I’ll complete the second round within two weeks of receiving it, then answer your follow-up questions.

A few practical details

What counts as two rounds?

A critique of your original manuscript, followed by one critique of your revised version of the same story.

What should I send?

A text manuscript in Word format (.docx). You don’t need illustrations to have your picture book text critiqued. If your manuscript is longer than 1,000 words, ask me for a quote.

Can you look at my query or pitch?

Yes. A critique of one query letter or short pitch is available as a $50 add-on.
